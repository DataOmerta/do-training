Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how D. Paraschidis Training Services ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the DO Training platform at dataomerta.github.io/do-training or the DO Training Android app.

Information We Collect

When you book a training session, we collect the following information you provide voluntarily:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number (optional)
  • Organisation name (optional)
  • Selected training modules and preferred session format
  • Notes or special requirements you choose to include

We do not collect payment information through this platform. Payments are arranged separately and directly.

How We Use Your Information

  • To confirm and manage your training booking
  • To send you session details including a Google Meet link
  • To issue a certificate of participation after your session
  • To notify you of any changes to your booking

Data Storage

Booking information is stored in your browser's local storage on your device and is not transmitted to any external server unless you submit a booking form, in which case booking details are sent to the trainer's email via EmailJS (emailjs.com) for the sole purpose of confirming your session.

Third-Party Services

  • EmailJS (emailjs.com) — used to send booking notification and confirmation emails. Their privacy policy applies to email transmission.
  • Google Meet — a unique meeting link is generated for online sessions. Google's privacy policy applies to use of Meet.
  • Google Fonts — loaded from fonts.googleapis.com for typography. Google's privacy policy applies.
  • ipapi.co — used once on page load to detect your country for language auto-selection. No personal data is stored from this request.

Cookies and Local Storage

This platform uses browser localStorage to remember your language preference, theme setting, and admin login state (admin users only). No tracking cookies are used. No advertising cookies are present.

Your Rights (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Union or European Economic Area, you have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us at gds_gr@hotmail.gr. We will respond within 30 days.

Data Retention

Booking data stored in your local browser remains until you clear your browser data. Emails received by the trainer are retained for a maximum of 2 years for business record purposes.

Children

This platform is designed for adult professionals and organisations. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. The educational content about child safety is informational in nature and directed at adult educators, parents, and professionals.
